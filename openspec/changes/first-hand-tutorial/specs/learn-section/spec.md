## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tutorial link on the course index
The `/learn` index SHALL show a link to `/tutorial` for newcomers ("New to riichi mahjong? Play your first game"),
above the list of lessons, as part of the server-rendered HTML.

#### Scenario: Newcomer on the course index
- **WHEN** a reader opens `/learn`
- **THEN** they see a link to `/tutorial` above the first lesson group
