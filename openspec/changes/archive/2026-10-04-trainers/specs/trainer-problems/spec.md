## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Problems are seeded and deterministic
A problem SHALL be generated by a pure function of (trainer, level, seed) using only the engine's public API and its
seeded RNG, never `Math.random`. The same input SHALL give the same position on the server and in the browser, on
every run. A generator SHALL return a Learn exercise (a `Position` plus the exercise kind and goal), so answers and
feedback come from the existing engine-backed goal and feedback functions.

#### Scenario: Server and browser agree
- **WHEN** the server renders a problem for seed `k3f9` and the browser hydrates it
- **THEN** both build the same hand with the same right answers

### Requirement: Realistic hands from self-play
Efficiency, Normal waits, yaku and score problems SHALL come from games played by the engine's bots from the seed.
A problem SHALL be one seat at a moment in that game, rebuilt with that seat as the reader: the first turn of a seat
without calls whose hand fits the level (efficiency), the first closed tenpai hand right after its discard (waits),
or the hand's win (yaku, score). Other seats' concealed tiles SHALL never be shown.

#### Scenario: Efficiency hand
- **WHEN** an Easy efficiency problem is generated
- **THEN** it is a closed 14-tile hand a seat held on its turn in a bot game, one tile from tenpai

### Requirement: Rebuilt positions are faithful
A problem built from a game moment SHALL be rebuilt as a `Position` (hand, melds, winning tile and how it came,
seat and round wind, dealer, riichi, dora and ura indicators, and other seats' quads, which reveal kan dora) that the
engine scores exactly as in the game. Wins whose value depends on what a rebuild cannot hold (ippatsu, haitei, houtei, rinshan, chankan, first-turn
wins) SHALL be skipped. Efficiency problems SHALL include no discards, so improving tiles are counted against the
hand and the dora indicator only.

#### Scenario: Same value
- **WHEN** a bot game ends in a riichi pinfu ron and a score problem is built from it
- **THEN** the rebuilt position's value (han, fu, points) equals the value the game paid

#### Scenario: Ippatsu skipped
- **WHEN** the next win in the generating game is an ippatsu tsumo
- **THEN** that win is skipped and generation continues

### Requirement: Only real decisions
Generators SHALL reject problems that test nothing:
- efficiency: hands where every discard that keeps the lowest shanten has the same improving count, or (Normal and
  Hard) where the only best discard is an isolated honor;
- waits (Normal): at most one problem in three may have a single winning kind;
- yaku: at least half of problems SHALL have two or more yaku (dora not counted).

#### Scenario: All ties rejected
- **WHEN** a candidate efficiency hand has every lowest-shanten discard at 20 improving tiles
- **THEN** it is skipped and the generator moves to the next candidate

### Requirement: One-suit waits
One-suit waits problems SHALL be 13 tiles of one numbered suit (at most four copies of a kind) drawn from the seed,
kept only when tenpai on at least three kinds. Suits SHALL vary between problems.

#### Scenario: Many-sided wait
- **WHEN** a One suit problem is generated
- **THEN** the hand is a single suit and has at least three winning kinds

### Requirement: Bounded generation
Every generator SHALL find a problem within a fixed budget of games or candidates, and SHALL fall back to the next
seed derivation (not loop forever or throw) when one game holds no fitting moment. Generating one problem SHALL take
well under the time of a page request on the server.

#### Scenario: Budget
- **WHEN** a seed's first game holds no Hard efficiency hand
- **THEN** the generator continues with the next game from the same seed and returns a problem

### Requirement: Seed sweep test
A test SHALL generate many problems per trainer and level and check: generation is deterministic, every problem has
at least one right answer by the engine, the filters hold, rebuilt wins score as in their game, and generation stays
within its budget. It SHALL be sanity-checked with planted bugs (mutation check).

#### Scenario: Broken filter caught
- **WHEN** the all-ties filter is disabled
- **THEN** the seed sweep test fails
