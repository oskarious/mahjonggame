chess.com, the pattern we follow elsewhere: **rated puzzles** (own rating vs puzzle rating, hints forfeit points),
**Puzzle Rush** (3 or 5 min or survival, three strikes, difficulty climbs, unrated), Puzzle Battle, a **Daily
Puzzle** with a streak, themed unrated practice.

## How often each drill appears

1. Efficiency / nanikiru: nearly everywhere. 14 tiles, tap a discard, then a ranked table of every discard's ukeire
   and tiles with yours vs the best; difficulty by shanten.
2. Scoring (han / fu / points), points the hardest form.
3. Waits (pick every winning tile; flushes as the hard tier).
4. Defense / safest tile vs riichi.
5. Shanten counting, tenpai recognition.
6. Yaku identification as a real drill (not flashcards) is rare: a gap.

## Take

- Copy: tap the tile; the ranked ukeire table with your pick marked; reject hands where every discard ties; one-line
  key point; a daily shared set with a shareable line; streaks without an account; suit swaps.
- Avoid: reveal-only answers, scores that claim more than they measure, desktop settings walls.
- Our edge: engine-graded answers with engine-written explanations (the Learn feedback), one-handed phone input that
  is the real game's, and the whole path (course → trainers → rated play) on one site. No riichi tool has a real
  puzzle rating yet: a natural follow-up for us.
