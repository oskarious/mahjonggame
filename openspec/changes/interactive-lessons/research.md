Condensed from a web survey. riichi.wiki returned 403 and the EMA 2025 PDF could not be extracted, so EMA
details come from secondary sources. Search rankings come from a non-Google search tool and are approximate.

## Typical beginner curriculum (consensus order)

1. Goal (4 sets + a pair; show a winning hand first). 2. Tiles: man/pin/sou (characters/circles/bamboo), winds,
dragons, simples/terminals/honors, red fives. 3. Sets: sequence, triplet, quad, pair; seven pairs, thirteen orphans.
4. Table, dealer, wall, dead wall, turn flow, river. 5. Calls (chii only from the left, pon/kan from anyone, priority),
open vs closed. 6. Winning: tsumo/ron, **a yaku is required**. 7. Tenpai/shanten. 8. Waits. 9. Core yaku (riichi,
tsumo, tanyao, yakuhai, pinfu) then common ones, then yakuman. 10. Riichi procedure. 11. Dora (indicator + 1, wrap),
kan dora, ura. 12. Furiten (discard, temporary, riichi). 13. Draws, noten payments, dealer repeat. 14. Scoring: han,
fu, limits, dealer ×1.5, tsumo vs ron, honba, riichi sticks. 15. Game end, placement, uma. 16. Strategy: efficiency,
when to call, defense (genbutsu → suji → kabe).

## Terminology

Japanese romaji is the norm (pon/chii/kan, tsumo/ron, riichi, tenpai, furiten, yaku, han/fu, dora); suits are
man/pin/sou glossed once as characters/circles/bamboo. Rulebooks (EMA/WRC) use English yaku names with Japanese
alongside. Use: Japanese term + one-time English gloss + a glossary.

## What confuses beginners

- "I had a complete hand, why can't I win?" → no yaku (esp. after calling) or furiten (all three kinds).
- Calling kills riichi/pinfu and often leaves no yaku; tenpai needs no yaku but winning does.
- Furiten covers the whole hand, not one tile.
- Too many yaku at once (start with riichi, tanyao, yakuhai, honitsu).
- Scoring/fu feel impenetrable; teaching scoring before yaku backfires.
- Ruleset differences between apps (red fives, kuitan, abortive draws).

## Gaps we can fill

1. No interactive course based on EMA 2025 rules.
2. No guided, step-by-step fu builder; trainers just reveal the answer.
3. No interactive "can I win here? why not?" (yaku/furiten) check, though it is the top confusion.
4. Dora wrap-around, call priority, honba / riichi sticks / noten payments, placement and uma are barely taught
   interactively.
5. Efficiency trainers don't explain why.
6. Hardly anything is built for one-handed portrait phone use without an app or gacha.
